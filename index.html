Web · University

Chitkara University — main website redesign

2024·Code Brigade·Web · Responsive

A full redesign of Chitkara University's main website — a modern, user-friendly, and informative online presence built to enhance experience, streamline navigation, and effectively communicate the university's offerings to prospective students, current students, faculty, and alumni.

Project Goals

  • Improve UXCreate an intuitive, easy-to-navigate website for diverse user groups.
  • Enhance visual appealUpdate aesthetics to reflect modern design standards and align with the university's brand identity.
  • Comprehensive infoEnsure detailed, easily accessible information about programs, admissions, and campus life.
  • Support marketingBuild a platform that supports outreach efforts and attracts new students.

User Research

Extensive research ensured the redesigned site met the needs of a diverse audience. Stakeholder interviews with university staff, students, and alumni surfaced expectations and existing pain points. User surveys with prospective students and parents mapped their information needs and browsing behaviors. Competitor analysis of leading university websites identified best practices and opportunities to differentiate.

Key Features

  • Modern responsive designAdapts seamlessly across devices and screen sizes.
  • Intuitive navigationSimplified structure so users find information quickly.
  • Program listingsComprehensive details with course descriptions, faculty profiles, and career prospects.
  • Admissions portalUser-friendly section with clear guidance on the application process, deadlines, and requirements.
  • Campus lifeFacilities, student activities, and resources via virtual tours and testimonials.
  • News & eventsDynamic section keeping the community and visitors updated.

Design Process

Discovery & planning — collaborated closely with stakeholders to define goals, scope, and align with strategic objectives. Wireframing & prototyping — created wireframes and interactive prototypes emphasising a clean, organised structure for easy navigation. Testing & iteration — conducted usability testing with prospective students, current students, and faculty, refining iteratively. Final design & handoff — delivered specifications and a design system to support consistent dev implementation.

Challenges

Balancing diverse user needs
Different audiences (prospective students, current students, parents, alumni, faculty) needed different information surfaced.
Tailored content and navigation paths for each group so every user could find what they needed without friction.
Content overhaul
Existing content was sprawling and hard to scan.
Implemented a content management strategy that prioritised essential info and improved clarity.
Accessibility
An inclusive site required adhering to WCAG standards from day one.
Designed with accessibility as a constraint, not an afterthought — colour, type, and interaction patterns all WCAG-tested.

Results & Impact

Enhanced UX — improved navigation, responsiveness, and accessibility led to increased user satisfaction. Higher engagement — dynamic content and interactive features contributed to higher visitor engagement. Positive feedback from users, stakeholders, and external audiences praised the modern design and usability. Enrolment support — supported university marketing efforts, leading to increased inquiries and applications.

Platform · UX

Alma Circle — alumni engagement platform

2024·Code Brigade·Web Platform

A comprehensive platform designed to enhance alumni engagement through two integrated modules — email campaigns and a social network — strengthening relationships between alumni and their alma mater via effective communication and interactive engagement.

Problem Statement

Alumni engagement often declines post-graduation due to limited interaction opportunities and outdated communication methods. Existing platforms lack integration and usability, making it challenging for institutions to maintain active alumni relationships. The challenge was to build a unified platform that seamlessly integrates email campaigns and social engagement to enhance connectivity and participation.

Design Decisions

  • Integrated modulesEmail and social network unified into a cohesive admin experience — manage communication and engagement from one place.
  • User-friendly UIClean, intuitive interface caters to admins of varying technical proficiency.
  • Customisation & templatesCustomisable email templates and social post designs maintain brand consistency while enabling quick communication.
  • Interactive featuresPolls and surveys embedded in the social module encourage active participation and feedback.

Key Features

Email campaign management — create and schedule campaigns, design templates, manage contact lists with segmentation. Social network engagement — post polls, announcements, event updates, share multimedia, facilitate alumni discussions and networking. Analytics & reporting — track campaign open rates, click-throughs, and engagement metrics on social posts (likes, comments, shares). User management — admin controls for roles and permissions; alumni profile management with privacy settings.

Design Process

Research & discovery — interviewed alumni relations administrators and alumni to surface communication pain points. Prototyping & iteration — built prototypes for both modules, refined through stakeholder feedback and usability testing. Visual design — established a professional but engaging identity reflecting institutional branding while appealing to alumni. Development & implementation — collaborated closely with engineering on backend integration, performance, and security.

Results & Impact

Increased engagement — alumni now participate actively in polls, discussions, and events, fostering community. Improved communication — administrators efficiently manage email and social activities from a single platform. Enhanced brand loyalty — alumni feel more connected through regular updates and engaging content, strengthening institutional ties.

Product · Blockchain

iCertify — blockchain credential validation

2023·Code Brigade·Web · Security

An innovative platform that authenticates academic and professional credentials — mark sheets, degrees, diplomas — using blockchain. iCertify provides a secure, transparent, tamper-proof method for validating credentials, addressing growing document fraud while making blockchain accessible.

Problem Statement

In the age of digital transformation, fraudulent credentials have become a significant issue, undermining trust in academic and professional qualifications. Traditional verification is slow, inefficient, and susceptible to tampering. The challenge was to design a platform that uses blockchain to provide secure, transparent, user-friendly credential verification — without scaring users with the complexity of the underlying technology.

Stanford Design Thinking Process

  • 1. EmpathizeStakeholder interviews with institutions, employers, and individuals; user personas; market analysis of existing verification methods.
  • 2. DefineIdentified core needs — tamper-proof issuance, quick verification, user-friendly UI for a complex underlying tech, robust privacy controls.
  • 3. IdeateBlockchain integration for data integrity, a personal dashboard for individuals, verification tools for employers, certificate template designer, bulk award flows.
  • 4. PrototypeWireframes and high-fidelity prototypes covering issuance/storage, verification/validation, and the personal dashboard.
  • 5. TestMultiple rounds of usability testing with institutions and employers; iterative refinements based on UI, navigation, and ease-of-use feedback.

Key Components

  • Credential issuanceInstitutions design, issue, store, and award credentials on a secure blockchain — a permanent, tamper-proof record.
  • Verification & validationEmployers and entities quickly and securely verify the authenticity of credentials.
  • User dashboardIndividuals manage and share their credentials securely from a personal dashboard.
  • Bulk awardsInstitutions issue certificates in bulk — a common need that's painful with paper systems.

Challenges

Blockchain complexity
Users had to understand and trust the underlying blockchain system without being designers, developers, or crypto-native.
Designed a clear, intuitive interface that demystified blockchain — simple explanations, contextual guides, no technical jargon in core flows.
Data security & privacy
Sensitive credential data needed protection while maintaining transparency that blockchain promises.
Implemented robust encryption and access controls; gave users granular control over their credential visibility.
Integration with existing systems
Academic and professional systems already in production — adoption depended on fitting into them.
Designed flexible API patterns and integration tools allowing easy connectivity with institutional and corporate platforms.

Results & Impact

Enhanced trust & security — the blockchain system delivered unparalleled trust in the validation process. Streamlined verification — institutions and employers saved significant time and reduced fraud risk through instant verification. Positive user feedback — users appreciated the platform's ease of use, transparency, and peace of mind regarding credential integrity.

SaaS · ERP

Schoolpad — SaaS ERP for schools

2023·Code Brigade·Web · Multi-role

A comprehensive SaaS-based ERP designed to meet the diverse needs of schools — a digital one-stop shop streamlining administrative and operational processes for teachers, students, parents, and administrative staff, from fee payments and admissions to inventory and transport coordination.

Problem Statement

Schools face numerous challenges managing administrative and operational tasks efficiently. Traditional methods are fragmented, time-consuming, and error-prone. The challenge was designing a unified ERP that addresses these pain points across a wide range of users — teachers, students, parents, and administrators — with a seamless and user-friendly experience for each.

Double Diamond Process

  • DiscoverInterviews and focus groups with all four user types; detailed personas and usage scenarios; competitor analysis of existing school management systems.
  • DefineSurfaced needs around fee payment, admissions, student profiles, inventory, transport, examinations, and analytics — each with distinct workflows.
  • DevelopWireframes and user flows for each role; responsive design across web and mobile; role-specific interfaces; interactive prototypes for user testing.
  • DeliverDetailed specifications, a comprehensive design system, and close collaboration with developers through implementation.

Key Features

  • Fee paymentStreamlined online collection; parents pay online, administrators track and manage efficiently.
  • AdmissionsOnline applications, document submission, and tracking — smooth and transparent enrolment.
  • Student profilesPersonal details, academic records, attendance tracking — all in one place.
  • Inventory managementFrom classroom supplies to sports equipment — resources tracked and available when needed.
  • Transport coordinationBus schedules, routes, student pickup/drop-off — logistics handled.
  • Exams & performanceExam creation, scheduling, grading, and performance reporting for students and teachers.
  • Dashboards & analyticsInsights into school operations, student performance, and resource management for every stakeholder.

Challenges

Diverse user needs
Balancing the varied needs of students, parents, teachers, and administrators within one platform.
Designed distinct, role-specific interfaces and functionalities tailored to each user group's unique requirements.
Data security & privacy
Handling sensitive student data while complying with privacy expectations.
Robust security measures — encryption and access controls — protecting user data and maintaining privacy.
Scalability & flexibility
The platform needed to scale across schools of different sizes with different requirements.
Developed a modular, scalable architecture supporting easy customisation and expansion.

Results & Impact

Streamlined operations — automated administrative processes reduced staff workload and improved efficiency. Enhanced UX — an intuitive platform caters to every stakeholder, from students and parents to teachers and admins. Comprehensive insights — dashboards and reports enable better decision-making and resource management.

Agency Maison — home & virtual assistant app

2023·Code Brigade·Mobile · iOS & Android

A mobile app designed for Agency Maison — three decades of holistic home management experience — bridging clients and their assigned managers. The app facilitates seamless communication and provides a comprehensive list of appointments, all within a simple and modern user interface for high-net-worth families.

Objective

Enhance the client experience with an intuitive, efficient platform for managing home services and executive assistant tasks. Facilitate communication — easy direct messaging between clients and assigned managers. Streamline appointments — a clear, accessible list helping clients manage schedules. Ensure usability — a simple, modern interface that offers a smooth and enjoyable experience.

User Research

Comprehensive research informed every design decision. Client interviews with current and prospective clients surfaced needs, preferences, and pain points. Stakeholder workshops with Agency Maison's staff captured internal processes and client interaction patterns. Competitive analysis of similar apps identified best practices and gaps to address.

Key Features

  • Chat functionalityReal-time messaging with assigned managers — notifications, history, persistent support.
  • Appointment managementDetailed list of upcoming appointments with add, edit, and reminder options.
  • User-friendly UIClean, simple, modern design prioritising ease of use and accessibility.

Design Process

Discovery & planning — defined scope through discussions with stakeholders and clients; focused on the unique needs of high-net-worth individuals seeking home management. Wireframing & prototyping — minimalist design ensuring a clutter-free, intuitive experience. User testing & iteration — usability testing with representative users; iterative improvements based on feedback. Final handoff — detailed specifications and a comprehensive style guide for design consistency.

Challenges

Seamless communication
Real-time messaging between clients and managers had to feel native and reliable.
Robust chat with real-time messaging, notifications, and history tracking so clients always feel connected.
Effective appointment management
Clients needed an intuitive way to manage and track schedules across busy lives.
Clear appointment system with calendar integration, reminders, and easy editing — keeping the cognitive load low.
Simple, modern aesthetic
Balancing rich functionality with a clean look appropriate for premium clientele.
Minimalist approach — generous whitespace, essential features only, no decorative clutter.

Results & Impact

Enhanced client communication — seamless real-time messaging improved overall client satisfaction. Streamlined appointment management — intuitive scheduling led to better organisation and fewer missed appointments. Positive user feedback — praise for the simple, modern design resulted in high adoption and engagement.

Web · Platform

My Online College — college decision platform

2023·Code Brigade·Web · EdTech

A dynamic web platform helping students explore and choose from a wide range of colleges and programs. The site empowers students to make informed decisions through detailed information and comparison tools — a one-stop resource for college discovery, comparison, and admission planning.

Problem Statement

Prospective college students face a daunting selection process — overwhelming information, the need to compare tuition, program quality, campus facilities, and student experiences. The challenge was designing a user-friendly platform that addresses these pain points, making the college selection process manageable and informed.

Double Diamond Process

  • DiscoverSurveys and interviews with students, parents, and educational counsellors; user personas mapping decision journeys; competitive analysis of existing comparison sites.
  • DefineSurfaced needs for comprehensive listings, advanced search/filtering, side-by-side comparison, detailed profiles, and engaging content.
  • DevelopWireframes and flows defining structure and functionality; responsive design for web and mobile; intuitive layout enabling easy navigation.
  • DeliverDetailed specifications, comprehensive style guide and component library, close dev collaboration through implementation.

Key Features

  • College listingsComprehensive directory categorised by location, field of study, and other criteria.
  • Advanced searchSearch and filter colleges by tuition, program duration, student reviews, and more.
  • Comparison toolSide-by-side comparison letting students evaluate colleges based on personal priorities.
  • Detailed profilesIn-depth college profiles covering campus, academic reputation, extracurriculars, and alumni success.
  • Admission guidanceResources for applications, deadlines, required documents, and personal statement tips.
  • ReviewsReviews and testimonials from current students and alumni for real-world insights.
  • Interactive contentVideos, virtual campus tours, and interactive content for richer exploration.

Design Decisions

  • In favour of searchImplemented robust search and filtering so users quickly find matches — enhancing platform usability.
  • Against overloadConcise, user-friendly profiles surfacing essentials first, with the option to dig deeper — preventing information overload.
  • In favour of interactivityVideos and virtual tours kept users engaged, providing a richer exploration.
  • Against complex navClean, intuitive layout — users find and compare colleges without unnecessary complexity.

Challenges

Diverse user preferences
Students arrive with very different educational goals, backgrounds, and priorities.
Flexible search and filtering options, with personalised recommendations based on user profiles and preferences.
Information overload
Balancing comprehensive information without overwhelming users.
Clear, concise college profiles and comparison tools, with progressive disclosure for those who want detail.
Sustained engagement
Keeping users motivated through what is often a months-long decision process.
Interactive content — videos, virtual tours — combined with user-friendly navigation and engaging design elements.

Results & Impact

Improved decision-making — students made informed, confident college choices through comprehensive accessible information. Enhanced user engagement — increased satisfaction with the interactive, user-friendly design. Positive feedback — praise for the intuitive interface and valuable comparison tools.
