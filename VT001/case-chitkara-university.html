Case study · 2024

Chitkara University

A unified, scalable, user-centered website built to communicate a complex institution clearly.

ClientChitkara University, India
RoleUI/UX Designer · Systems Lead
Duration~4 months
ServicesUX Research · UI Design · Design System · CMS Strategy
C
— Overview

The project

Chitkara University offers a wide array of programs across schools and disciplines. The website needed to communicate that breadth to multiple audiences — prospective students, current students, parents, faculty, alumni — without overwhelming any of them.

— The problem

Where it started

The legacy site had grown over time into a tangle of pages, programs, and broken hierarchies. Prospective students couldn't find what they needed in under two clicks. Marketing couldn't ship updates without engineering. The brand felt dated against more progressive peer institutions.

— Approach

How I thought about it

Audience-led IA. Distinct journeys for prospective, current, and faculty users — each entering through the same homepage but routed to relevance fast. Modular CMS components so marketing owns publishing. Accessibility baked into the system, not bolted on.

— Process

Step by step

_01 Discovery Stakeholder interviews across academics, marketing, and admissions. User surveys with 200+ prospective students. Competitor audit of 12 leading universities.
_02 Information architecture Card-sorted with three user groups. Reduced top-level navigation from 9 to 5 items. Built a faceted program-finder as the conversion-critical surface.
_03 Wireframes & prototypes Low-fi → mid-fi in Figma. Interactive program-finder prototype tested with 18 prospective students before any pixel was polished.
_04 Design system Tokens, components, page templates — built so marketing could compose new pages without engineering tickets.
_05 Test & ship Two rounds of usability testing. Final WCAG AA audit. Phased rollout with engineering across one academic cycle.
— Key features

What shipped

Responsive system that adapts cleanly from mobile to large desktops
Faceted program finder with smart filters and saved searches
Admissions portal with clear application status, deadlines, and document checklist
Virtual campus tour module with hotspot-driven exploration
Live news & events feed driven by editorial CMS
Accessibility — keyboard-first navigation, ARIA-correct components, AA contrast everywhere
— Challenges

The hard parts

Diverse audiences, one homepage

Solved with audience-aware routing — three quick-entry paths visible above the fold, never forcing a generic experience on anyone.

Legacy content overhaul

Built a content audit framework. Marketing and academics co-owned the migration. Cut total page count by 38% without losing critical info.

Accessibility at scale

Made it a system constraint, not a checklist. Every token, component, and template passes AA by construction.

Scalability for growth

Modular template architecture. New programs and schools added by config, not by custom dev.

— Outcomes

What it moved

Prospective inquiries +42% YoY
Time-to-program-page −61%
Mobile bounce rate −28%
WCAG compliance AA verified
— Reflection
Higher-ed sites tend to drift into committee compromise — every department wants their voice on the homepage. The win on this one was holding the line that prospective students are the primary audience and everything else is secondary navigation. Once that decision was protected, every downstream choice got easier.
