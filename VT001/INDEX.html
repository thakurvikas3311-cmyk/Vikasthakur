Frame — 01 / Designer at rest

I make products that feel as good as they work.

I'm a UI/UX designer with nearly three years shipping interfaces for web, mobile, and platform products. I care most about clarity, accessibility, and how something feels in someone's hands.

Day-to-day I own design end-to-end — framing the problem, journey mapping, prototyping, maintaining the design system, and shipping iteratively alongside engineering and marketing.

Off the clock I cut motion and video in Premiere Pro for brand work, and I push new AI tools into my craft pipeline whenever they earn their keep.

FAQ /s.

What kind of work are you taking on in 2026?

+
Product UI/UX — web and mobile — with a soft spot for systems thinking, design-system stewardship, and dev handoffs that don't fall apart. Full-time, contract, and select freelance.

Do you work remotely or only in India?

+
Both. Based in Chandigarh (UTC +5:30), comfortable overlapping with EU and US hours. Onsite within India when the brief deserves it.

What does your design process look like?

+
Frame → Sketch → Refine → Ship. Research-led at the start, system-led in the middle, polish-led at the end. Iterative, in Agile sprints, alongside engineering — never thrown over the wall.

Which tools do you use day-to-day?

+
Figma is the daily driver. Framer and Webflow for prototypes and marketing surfaces. Premiere Pro for motion. Claude, Google Stitch, and other AI tools when they earn their seat at the table.

How do we start a project together?

+
Email a short brief — goals, audience, scope, timeline. I'll come back within a day with a call slot and an honest read on whether we're a fit.

Can I see longer case studies?

+
Yes. Full case studies for each project on request — problem framing, research, decisions made, outcomes shipped.
